**Position:** Graduate Teaching Assistant  
**Duration:** January 2022 – March 2025  
**Institution:** Virginia Tech, Department of Civil Engineering

## Courses Taught

### Traffic Engineering
- **Level:** Undergraduate and Graduate students
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Mentored students during office hours through one-to-one communication
  - Graded assignments and provided detailed feedback
  - Demonstrated traffic analysis procedures using Highway Capacity Manual (HCM)
  - Provided clarification on complex traffic engineering concepts

### Transportation Planning and Land Use
- **Level:** Undergraduate and Graduate students  
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Conducted office hours for student mentoring and support
  - Graded coursework and assignments
  - Assisted students with transportation planning methodologies
  - Facilitated understanding of land use and transportation interactions

## Key Teaching Activities

**Traffic Simulation Modeling**
- Demonstrated traffic simulation modeling techniques to students
- Provided hands-on training with industry-standard software
- Helped students understand practical applications of simulation tools

**Traffic Analysis Procedures**
- Taught students how to apply Highway Capacity Manual (HCM) procedures
- Demonstrated real-world traffic analysis applications
- Guided students through complex traffic engineering calculations

**Student Mentoring**
- Provided individual support through office hours
- Assisted students with coursework questions and concept clarification
- Supported both undergraduate and graduate student learning objectives

## Skills Developed
- Effective communication of complex technical concepts
