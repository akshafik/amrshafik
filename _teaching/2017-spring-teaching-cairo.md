**Position:** Graduate Teaching Assistant  
**Duration:** January 2017 – August 2021  
**Institution:** Cairo University, Faculty of Engineering

## Teaching Responsibilities

### Course Support Activities
- **Assignment Demonstration:** Guided undergraduate students through complex engineering problems and coursework assignments
- **Problem Solving:** Provided detailed solutions and explanations for course-related problems
- **Assessment:** Checked assignments, proctored examinations, and provided grades according to university standards
- **Documentation:** Maintained comprehensive records of attendance and assignment completion for all students

### Curriculum Development
- **Lesson Preparation:** Developed lesson plans according to course outlines to effectively convey required material
- **Educational Enhancement:** Created supplementary materials to deepen student understanding of core engineering concepts
- **Content Delivery:** Ensured alignment between teaching materials and learning objectives

### Student Instruction and Mentoring
- **Small Group Teaching:** Conducted focused instruction sessions for small groups of students on specific coursework topics
- **Individual Support:** Provided one-on-one mentoring through office hours and personalized communication
- **Academic Guidance:** Supported students in understanding complex civil engineering concepts and methodologies

### Administrative Support
- **Faculty Assistance:** Supported faculty members with administrative tasks including:
  - Copying and organizing course materials
  - Collecting and organizing student assignments
  - Preparing materials for upcoming classes and examinations
- **Accreditation Support:** Contributed to institutional quality assurance by writing and submitting documentation and reports for accreditation processes

## Key Achievements
- Successfully mentored hundreds of undergraduate students throughout tenure
- Contributed to departmental accreditation documentation and reporting
- Developed effective teaching methodologies for civil engineering concepts
- Maintained high standards in student assessment and academic record keeping

## Skills Developed
- Comprehensive understanding of undergraduate civil engineering curriculum
- Advanced communication and presentation skills
- Student assessment and evaluation techniques
- Academic administration and documentation
- Mentoring and guidance methodologies
