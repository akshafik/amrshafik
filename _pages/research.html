Research Interests

My work focuses on traffic operations, intelligent transportation systems, connected and automated vehicles, and data-driven transportation planning.

Core Areas

Traffic Flow Theory

Understanding and modeling traffic dynamics across interrupted and uninterrupted facilities.

Traffic Control and Optimization

Developing operational strategies for safer, more efficient signalized networks.

Traffic Modeling and Simulation

Using microscopic and macroscopic simulation to evaluate transportation systems and policies.

Intelligent Transportation Systems

Applying sensing, communication, and automation to improve mobility outcomes.

Transportation Planning

Supporting demand modeling, network evaluation, and long-range transportation decisions.

Connected and Automated Vehicles

Optimizing vehicle trajectories and infrastructure interactions under uncertainty.
