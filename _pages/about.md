AAmr Shafik is currently a **PhD Candidate** in the Charles E. Via Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at **Virginia Tech**. He earned a **B.Sc. (Hons.) in Civil Engineering** from Cairo University, Cairo, Egypt, in 2016, and an **M.Sc. in Transportation Engineering** from Cairo University in 2020.  

Amr is currently a **Graduate Research Assistant** with the Center for Sustainable Mobility at the Virginia Tech Transportation Institute.  
His research interests span a wide range of topics, including:  
- Traffic control and optimization  
- Traffic modeling and simulation  
- Intelligent transportation systems  
- Transportation planning
